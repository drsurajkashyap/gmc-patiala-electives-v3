The counselling pathway

Five steps from login to final allotment

You can log in before your turn, monitor the queue and seat matrix, and prepare alternatives. Final selection becomes available only when your merit rank is active.

STEP 01

Login

Use your Roll Number as User ID and the password issued to you.

STEP 02

Follow the queue

See the active merit rank, students ahead and approximate waiting time.

STEP 03

Choose a timeslot

Select either First Timeslot or Second Timeslot when your turn opens.

STEP 04

Select A + B

Choose exactly one Block A and one Block B elective in the same timeslot.

STEP 05

Confirm once

Review both electives and the posting sequence before final confirmation.

Preference lookup

Prepare before your turn gets close.

When you are within 20 students of your counselling turn, the portal unlocks a preference lookup so you can check whether your preferred Block A + Block B combination is currently available together.

UNLOCKS WITHIN 20 STUDENTS OF YOUR TURN
Planning aid — not a reservationPreference lookup uses live seat availability but does not hold seats. Students ahead of you can still change the availability before your turn begins.
What to do

Before, during and after your turn

BEFORE YOUR TURN

Prepare alternatives

  • Keep your Roll Number and password ready.
  • Review multiple Block A and Block B preferences.
  • Watch live seat availability.
  • Stay on a reliable internet connection.
DURING YOUR TURN

Make one careful decision

  • Select one complete timeslot.
  • Select one Block A and one Block B elective.
  • Review both selections before confirming.
  • Do not refresh or repeatedly press Confirm during submission.
AFTER CONFIRMATION

Verify the final allotment

  • Check the allotted Block A and Block B electives.
  • Check the displayed posting sequence.
  • A successfully confirmed allotment is treated as final.
  • Keep the confirmation visible until details are verified.
Counselling rules

Fairness is enforced by the system

The portal is designed so merit order, live seat counts and final allocations remain authoritative at the backend.

01
Strict merit orderOnly the currently active merit rank can submit a final student allocation.
02
One complete timeslotBlock A and Block B must come from the same selected timeslot.
03
Live availabilitySeat counts change as earlier students complete counselling.
04
No reservation by lookup or draftSeats are consumed only after successful final confirmation.
05
Final means finalReview the timeslot and both electives carefully before confirming.
06
Timer expiry requires interventionIf your timer expires, wait for the counselling administrator. The system does not automatically skip you.
