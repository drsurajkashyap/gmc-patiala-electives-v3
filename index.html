How counselling works

The process is simple. Please read these steps before your turn.

1

Login

Use your Roll Number as User ID and the password issued to you.

2

Wait for your turn

Follow the current active merit rank, students ahead and live seat availability.

3

Select your timeslot

When your turn becomes active, choose either First Timeslot or Second Timeslot.

4

Select and confirm

Choose one Block A and one Block B elective in the same timeslot, then confirm once.

Timeslot Structure

Each timeslot is a complete Block A + Block B posting sequence.

Important: Block A and Block B must both be chosen within the same timeslot. You cannot mix one block from First Timeslot with the other from Second Timeslot.

Student Instructions

Please follow these instructions to keep counselling fast, accurate and fair for everyone.

Keep your login credentials private. Do not share your password or allow another student to submit choices from your account.

Before counselling

  • Use a phone or computer with stable internet and sufficient battery.
  • Keep your Roll Number/User ID and password ready.
  • Review preferred Block A and Block B electives in advance.
  • You may remain logged in while waiting for your merit turn.
  • Seat availability is live and may change as counselling progresses.

While you are waiting

  • You can see the current active Merit Rank and students ahead.
  • Approximate waiting time is shown while counselling is running.
  • Live seat availability remains visible for both timeslots.
  • Preference lookup becomes available when you are within 20 students of your turn.
  • Preference lookup does not reserve seats.

When your turn becomes active

  • A fixed counselling countdown is displayed.
  • Choose one timeslot.
  • Select exactly one Block A and one Block B elective.
  • Review both choices before final confirmation.
  • Do not refresh or repeatedly press Confirm while submission is processing.

After confirmation

  • Your final Block A and Block B allotments are shown on screen.
  • Your posting sequence is based on the selected timeslot.
  • A successfully confirmed allotment is treated as final.
  • If the timer expires, wait for administrator intervention.

Important Rules

These rules are enforced to preserve merit order and seat accuracy.

  • Counselling proceeds strictly in one global merit queue.
  • Only the currently active merit rank can submit a final allocation.
  • Each student selects one timeslot, one Block A elective and one Block B elective.
  • Both electives must belong to the same timeslot.
  • Seat availability shown in the portal is live.
  • Draft selections and preference lookup do not reserve seats.
  • Seats are consumed only after successful final confirmation.
  • Timer expiry does not automatically skip the student; administrator intervention is required.
